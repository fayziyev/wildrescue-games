English

Wild Rescue Zoo ("the game") is made by an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

What we do not collect. The game has no accounts, no chat and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, location or contacts, and we do not run our own servers.

Saved progress. Your zoo progress, chosen language and purchases are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.

Advertising. The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent first using Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from that form. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

In-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your card or payment details; we only receive a confirmation of what was bought.

Children. The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.

Contact. Questions: bidamfayz007@gmail.com
